# Code of Conduct

Nez-Code is a small project shared between friends. Keep it a place people
want to come back to.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept review feedback on the code, not the person.
- Help newcomers find their way around the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing someone else's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

The repository owner moderates issues, pull requests and discussions.
Report problems privately to the owner through GitHub. Depending on the
situation the owner may edit or remove content, or remove a contributor's
access to the repository.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
